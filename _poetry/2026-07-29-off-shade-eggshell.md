---
title: Off-shade Eggshell 
---
Have you ever considered your hands and thought:  
"how did that get there?"  
Markings  
the colour of a wolf moon  
peer back at you  
teasing your memory  
like a Burlesque house worker.  

The in-between of railroad spikes  
are scale-like  
after a hot shower  
reminding me that perhaps  
my old lovers had a point about using pale creams.  

Icebergs and quartz  
opposite warm, thick hide.  
Cold in the zenith    
of isolation   
yet still welcoming   
strangers, colleagues, acquaintances, friends, family  
into the skin.
