---
title: Hole
---
There is a dark spot   
growing in the centre of my sternum.   


It consumes the light    
meant for the hedonism    

that life represents.    
You left me like this,    

but in all honesty    
I have nothing with which to hate you.    

Do you know    
that I still think of you?    

Every day    
regret, pain, ache, longing    

but no means of comfort    
are available.    

Don't answer the question;     
I don't care    

for what you have to say.
