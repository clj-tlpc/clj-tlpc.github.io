---
title: Flannel
---
I own a single flannel.   

It is as stereotypically    
flannel as it gets,     

with its red and black checkerboard pattern,           
durability,           
and ability to effortlessly coexist with jeans          

I would be remiss to not mention         
that it stereotypes me        
as a Canadian        
although regrettably not a lumberjack.

Most importantly,         
it reminds me          
of the person who gave it to me             
and whenever I wear it                 
I can pretend she's still there.
